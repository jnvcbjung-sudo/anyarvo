01

They say the sun is something to avoid.
We think it's a backdrop to enjoy.

"Arvo" is Australian slang for afternoon. We named ourselves for the hottest hour of the day — 2 p.m. — because we believe even that moment should be one of your brightest.

Any Arvo · Any Afternoon

Most sun care treats daylight as the enemy — the cause of spots and ageing, a thing to escape. We saw it differently. Sunlight isn't something to run from; it's the beautiful backdrop to a life lived fully, out in the open. So we set out to make sun care for the people who don't come indoors early — the ones who live for the session.

That meant getting the fundamentals right and then some. Real protection, SPF50+ PA++++, with a grip that holds through sweat and long hours outside — and a feel light enough that reapplying becomes a pleasure, not a chore. We built it in three finishes, so it fits your skin, your sport, and your day: Breeze, Sand, and Terra.

And because we want these days to last, we made choices that respect the places they happen — reef-friendly formulas for the water you swim over, and care for the coast and the outdoors we all share. Leave the sun worry to us. You just keep on the sunny side.
